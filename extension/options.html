Demo Director

Veils

Each veil hides everything its selectors match. The slot number is the hotkey and Stream Deck slot. To add one, open your demo page in Rehearse mode and use Pick element from the toolbar popup or the Rehearse chip.

Segments (demo tabs)

Each segment is one tab of your run-of-show, with its own camera preset, talking points and time budget. The console shows the current segment's notes and timer. To set the camera spot, open the tab in Rehearse mode and drag the bubble (resize it from its orange handle). The tabs your demo needs, in order. Reset demo rebuilds your demo window (the last browser window you used) to exactly these tabs. It keeps or reopens each one at its starting URL, closes every other tab in that window, and focuses tab 1. Other windows and the console are left alone. A tab counts as a match if it's on the same site at or below the URL's path.

Demo Director runs on localhost:3333 and *.app.github.dev by default. For any other site (e.g. the storefront), click Allow on its row so the veils, camera, topics and Slack post work there too.

When screen sharing, share the window or screen, not a single tab, or the audience won't follow you between tabs.

Slides & speaker notes

Add your Google Slides deck as a demo tab (Present mode, published, or the editor) and load its notes here. When that tab is on screen, the Director console switches to Slides mode and shows the current slide's notes as you advance. On any other tab it's back to Demo mode.

Show slides and notes

    Camera bubble

    The bubble is drawn inside the shared tab, top-right. Grant access once here, since the extension asks for the camera and not each site. Background can replace the room behind you with a solid colour or a blur. It's done on your Mac with Google's MediaPipe person segmentation; nothing is uploaded.

    Topics (lower-thirds)

    Topic N = script step N. Shown bottom-left. Defaults come from topics.json.

    Slack notification

    A Slack-style post that slides in top-right, placed below the camera bubble automatically. Defaults come from slack-toast.json.

    Stream Deck

    The Demo Director Stream Deck plugin listens on your Mac at 127.0.0.1. The extension connects to it automatically and reconnects if either one restarts. Only change the port if something else on your Mac already uses it, and set the same port in the plugin.

    Back up and share

    Export your veils and settings (topics, Slack post, camera sizes) as one JSON file to commit to the repo or hand to a colleague. Importing replaces them.

    Keyboard shortcuts (fallback)

    The Stream Deck plugin is the main control. These shortcuts keep working if it isn't running. Chrome only lets an extension suggest 4 keys; set the rest yourself.
